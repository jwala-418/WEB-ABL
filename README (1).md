## Project Overview

This project contains three website design cases based on the supplied
screenshots:

1.  **ArenaX Gaming Website**
2.  **Online Library**
3.  **Foodie Restaurant**

Each case presents information in organized sections with a navigation
area, main content, and footer or ending section.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Case 1: ArenaX Gaming Website

## Aim

To create a gaming website homepage that presents popular games, gaming
tournaments, esports teams, and top players.

## Main Sections

-   **Header and Navigation:** ArenaX Gaming title and links for Home,
    Games, Events, Teams, Players, Ranking, News, and Contact.
-   **Hero Section:** "Enter the Gaming World" introduction with an
    "Explore Now" button.
-   **Popular Games:** Call of Duty, Clash of Clans, and Fortnite, each
    with a short description.
-   **Gaming Tournaments:** Tournament name, date, prize money,
    participant information, and registration status.
-   **Esports Teams:** Team names, games played, and member counts.
-   **Top Gamers:** Selected player names, games, and skill levels.
-   **Top Player Rankings:** A section for player ranking information.

## Purpose

The website gives visitors a central place to explore games, view
tournament information, and learn about esports teams and players.

# Case 2: Online Library

## Aim

To create an online library homepage where users can explore book
categories, search for books, filter the catalog, and check book
availability.

## Main Sections

-   **Header and Navigation:** Online Library title and links for Home,
    Books, Catalog, and Library Rules.
-   **Book Categories:** Programming, Science, Mathematics, History,
    Fiction, and Technology.
-   **Filter Books:** Category and availability filters.
-   **Search Books:** A search field for finding books by book name or
    author.
-   **Popular Books:** Book covers, titles, authors, and availability:
    -   *Introduction to Algorithms* --- Thomas H. Cormen --- Available.
    -   *Atomic Habits* --- James Clear --- Available.
    -   *The Alchemist* --- Paulo Coelho --- Unavailable.
-   **Book Catalog:** A table containing Book ID, Book Name, Author,
    Category, and Status.
-   **Library Rules:** Maintain silence, carry a library ID card, return
    books by the due date, do not damage books, and keep the library
    clean.
-   **Footer:** Copyright information.

## Purpose

The website organizes book information so visitors can search for books,
browse categories, and check whether a book is available.

# Case 3: Foodie Restaurant

## Aim

To create a restaurant website homepage that introduces the restaurant,
displays a special offer, presents popular dishes with prices, and
explains the restaurant's benefits.

## Main Sections

-   **Header:** The restaurant name "GOLD Restaurant" and a short
    food-delivery tagline.
-   **Navigation Bar:** Home, Menu, Reviews, and Contact links.
-   **Welcome Section:** Introduces the restaurant and describes its
    food and service.
-   **Special Offer:** A 20% discount on the first order.
-   **About Our Restaurant:** Describes the restaurant's focus on taste,
    quality, fresh ingredients, and customer service.
-   **Our Popular Dishes:** Food images, names, and prices:
    -   Cheese Pizza --- ₹190
    -   Veg Burger --- ₹89
    -   Grilled Special --- ₹240
    -   Chocolate Donut --- ₹70
-   **Why Choose Us?:** Fresh and quality ingredients, affordable
    prices, fast home delivery, and friendly customer service.
-   **Footer:** Copyright and thank-you messages.

## Purpose

The website helps visitors learn about the restaurant, view popular food
items and prices, and see the offers and services provided.

## Conclusion

The three website cases demonstrate different homepage layouts and
content organization:

-   **ArenaX Gaming Website:** Games, tournaments, esports teams, and
    player information.
-   **Online Library:** Book categories, searching, filtering,
    availability, catalog details, and library rules.
-   **Foodie Restaurant:** Restaurant information, offers, popular
    dishes, prices, and customer benefits.

Together, these cases demonstrate how navigation, content sections, and
a footer can organize information clearly on a website.
